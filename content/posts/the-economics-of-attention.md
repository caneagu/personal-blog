Attention is an economic resource. Teams that respect this tend to ship clearer products and communication.

## A simple framework

- Cost: How much cognition does this require?

- Return: What user value is created?

- Persistence: Does this save attention in the future?

Products that win tend to minimize upfront cognitive cost while compounding long-term understanding.
