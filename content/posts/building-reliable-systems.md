Reliability is usually treated as a late-stage optimization. That is a mistake.

## Three practical habits

1. Write down failure modes before launch.
2. Define rollback paths before deployment.
3. Monitor user-level outcomes, not just service metrics.

When uncertainty is high, resilience compounds faster than raw velocity.
